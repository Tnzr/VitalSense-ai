Guided training course

Six modules: heart & lung sound basics, source identification, screening, heart typing, lung typing, and a final assessment. Lessons include curated examples; practice rounds are graded against dataset ground truth.

Start the course

Listening game

Random listening rounds across five domains with instant explanations. A quick way to keep your ear in shape after the course.

Play the game

Analyze a recording

Upload or record one clip for a full report: heart condition typing (10 types), lung condition typing (6 types) and normal/abnormal screening, routed by sound source, plus an interactive log-mel explorer and a quantified audio report. Upload several files for a batch table.

Tip: the dataset clips are 4 kHz mono WAV. Upload one for a full report, many for a batch table.

Quiet room; turn off music/TV. Press the phone's bottom-edge mic gently against the chest (heart: left of the sternum; lungs: upper back) and stay still. The models were trained on digital-stethoscope clips, so phone audio is exploratory.
